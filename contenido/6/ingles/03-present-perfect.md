## ¿Cuándo se usa el present perfect?

Para hablar de **experiencias** en la vida (sin decir cuándo) o de acciones pasadas que tienen relación con el **presente**:

- *I **have visited** London.* (He visitado Londres — alguna vez en mi vida.)
- *She **has lost** her keys.* (Ha perdido las llaves — y ahora no las tiene.)

## Cómo se forma

**have / has + participio**

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|
| I/You/We/They **have** ('ve) played | **haven't** played | **Have** you played? |
| He/She/It **has** ('s) played | **hasn't** played | **Has** she played? |

**Respuestas cortas:** *Have you finished? → Yes, I **have**. / No, I **haven't**.*

## El participio (past participle)

- **Verbos regulares:** igual que el pasado, con **-ed**: *play → played*, *visit → visited*.
- **Verbos irregulares:** hay que aprenderlos (es la **tercera columna**).

| Infinitivo | Pasado | Participio |
|---|---|---|
| be | was/were | **been** |
| go | went | **gone** / **been** |
| do | did | **done** |
| see | saw | **seen** |
| eat | ate | **eaten** |
| write | wrote | **written** |
| take | took | **taken** |
| make | made | **made** |
| have | had | **had** |
| fly | flew | **flown** |
| swim | swam | **swum** |
| break | broke | **broken** |

## Ever, never, already, yet, just

| Palabra | Significado | Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **ever** | alguna vez (preguntas) | *Have you **ever** been to Paris?* |
| **never** | nunca | *I have **never** eaten sushi.* |
| **already** | ya (afirmativas) | *I have **already** done my homework.* |
| **yet** | todavía (negativas) / ya (preguntas), al **final** | *I haven't finished **yet**.* / *Have you finished **yet**?* |
| **just** | acabar de | *She has **just** arrived.* (Acaba de llegar.) |

> ⚠️ **Present perfect o past simple:** si decimos **cuándo** pasó (*yesterday, last year, in 2020*), usamos el **past simple**: *I **visited** London **last year**.* Si no decimos cuándo, el **present perfect**: *I **have visited** London.*

# Examen

? Completa: She ___ lost her keys.
+ has
- have
- is
- did
> Con she se usa has.

? ¿Cuál es el participio de "see"?
= seen
> See → saw → seen.

? ¿Cuál es el participio de "write"?
+ written
- wrote
- writed
- writing
> Write → wrote → written.

? Completa: Have you ___ been to Paris?
+ ever
- never
- yet
- just
> Ever = alguna vez, en preguntas.

? Completa: I haven't finished my homework ___.
= yet
> Yet va al final en las negativas: todavía no.

? "She has just arrived" significa "Ella acaba de llegar".
= verdadero
> Just = acabar de.

? Elige la oración correcta.
+ I visited London last year.
- I have visited London last year.
- I has visited London last year.
- I visit London last year.
> Si decimos cuándo (last year), se usa el past simple.

? Respuesta corta: "Has he finished?" — "No, he ___."
= hasn't | hasnt | hasn't | has not
> Yes, he has. / No, he hasn't.

? ¿Cuál es el participio de "go"?
+ gone
- went
- goed
- going
> Go → went → gone (o been).

? "I have never eat sushi" es correcto.
= falso
> Se usa el participio: I have never eaten sushi.
