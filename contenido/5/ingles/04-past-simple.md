## ¿Cuándo se usa el past simple?

Para hablar de **acciones terminadas** en el **pasado**:

- *I **visited** my grandparents **last Sunday**.*
- *We **went** to the beach **yesterday**.*

## Verbos regulares: + -ed

| Regla | Ejemplos |
|---|---|
| Normalmente se añade **-ed** | *play → played*, *watch → watched*, *walk → walked* |
| Acaban en **-e**: solo **-d** | *dance → danced*, *like → liked* |
| Consonante + **-y** → **-ied** | *study → studied*, *cry → cried* |
| Vocal + consonante (cortos) → se **dobla** | *stop → stopped*, *plan → planned* |

> 💡 El past simple es **igual para todas las personas**: *I played, she played, they played*.

## Verbos irregulares

No siguen la regla: hay que **aprenderlos de memoria**.

| Infinitivo | Pasado | Significado |
|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | ser, estar |
| go | **went** | ir |
| have | **had** | tener |
| do | **did** | hacer |
| eat | **ate** | comer |
| drink | **drank** | beber |
| see | **saw** | ver |
| come | **came** | venir |
| get | **got** | conseguir, llegar |
| make | **made** | hacer, fabricar |
| take | **took** | coger, llevar |
| buy | **bought** | comprar |
| write | **wrote** | escribir |
| read | **read** (se pronuncia "red") | leer |
| swim | **swam** | nadar |
| run | **ran** | correr |
| sing | **sang** | cantar |
| say | **said** | decir |
| give | **gave** | dar |
| find | **found** | encontrar |

## Negativa: didn't + verbo en forma básica

- *I **didn't play** football.* (¡no "didn't played"!)
- *She **didn't go** to school.* (¡no "didn't went"!)

## Preguntas: Did + sujeto + verbo en forma básica

- ***Did** you **watch** the film?* → *Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**.*
- *Where **did** you **go**?* → *I went to London.*

> ⚠️ Con **didn't** y **did**, el verbo vuelve a su **forma básica**: el pasado ya lo indica *did*.

# Examen

? ¿Cuál es el pasado de "play"?
= played
> Verbo regular: se añade -ed.

? ¿Cuál es el pasado de "go"?
+ went
- goed
- gone
- goes
> Es un verbo irregular.

? ¿Cuál es el pasado de "study"?
+ studied
- studyed
- studed
- studies
> Consonante + y → -ied.

? ¿Cuál es el pasado de "eat"?
= ate
> Eat → ate (irregular).

? Elige la negativa correcta.
+ She didn't go to school.
- She didn't went to school.
- She don't went to school.
- She wasn't go to school.
> Con didn't, el verbo va en forma básica.

? Elige la pregunta correcta.
+ Did you watch the film?
- Did you watched the film?
- Do you watched the film?
- You did watch the film?
> Did + sujeto + verbo en forma básica.

? ¿Cuál es el pasado de "buy"?
+ bought
- buyed
- brought
- buied
> Buy → bought.

? Respuesta corta: "Did you see the match?" — "No, I ___."
= didn't | didnt | didn't | did not
> Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

? El past simple de los verbos regulares es igual para todas las personas.
= verdadero
> I played, you played, he played…

? "Yesterday I swimmed in the sea" es correcto.
= falso
> Swim es irregular: Yesterday I swam in the sea.
