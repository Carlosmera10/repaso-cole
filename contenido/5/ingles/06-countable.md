## Contables e incontables

- **Contables (countable):** se pueden **contar**. Tienen singular y plural: *an apple → two apples*, *a banana → three bananas*.
- **Incontables (uncountable):** **no** se pueden contar por unidades. No tienen plural ni llevan *a/an*: *water, milk, bread, rice, cheese, sugar, juice, money*.

> 💡 Para contar incontables usamos envases o medidas: *a **bottle of** water*, *a **glass of** milk*, *a **slice of** bread*, *a **piece of** cake*, *a **kilo of** rice*.

## a / an / some / any

| | Contables singular | Contables plural | Incontables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Afirmativa** | **a** / **an** | **some** | **some** |
| **Negativa** | **a** / **an** | **any** | **any** |
| **Pregunta** | **a** / **an** | **any** | **any** |

- *There's **an** orange.* (**an** delante de sonido vocal: *an apple, an egg*)
- *There are **some** eggs.* / *There's **some** milk.*
- *There aren't **any** eggs.* / *There isn't **any** milk.*
- *Are there **any** eggs?* / *Is there **any** milk?*

> ⚠️ Excepción: para **ofrecer** o **pedir** algo se usa **some** en preguntas: *Would you like **some** juice?*

## How much / How many

- **How many** + **contables en plural**: *How **many** apples are there?* (¿Cuántas manzanas hay?)
- **How much** + **incontables**: *How **much** water is there?* (¿Cuánta agua hay?)
- *How **much** is it?* = ¿Cuánto cuesta?

## Much / many / a lot of

- **many** → contables (sobre todo en negativas y preguntas): *There aren't **many** cars.*
- **much** → incontables (sobre todo en negativas y preguntas): *I don't have **much** time.*
- **a lot of** → con los dos, sobre todo en afirmativas: *There are **a lot of** people. There's **a lot of** noise.*
