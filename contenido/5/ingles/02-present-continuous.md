## ¿Cuándo se usa el present continuous?

Para hablar de lo que está pasando **ahora mismo**, en este momento:

- *I **am reading** a book now.* (Estoy leyendo un libro ahora.)
- *Look! It **is raining**.* (¡Mira! Está lloviendo.)

Palabras que lo acompañan: **now**, **right now**, **at the moment**, **Look!**, **Listen!**

## Cómo se forma

**am / is / are + verbo con -ing**

| Afirmativa | Negativa | Pregunta |
|---|---|---|
| I **am** (I'm) play**ing** | I **'m not** playing | **Am** I playing? |
| He/She/It **is** (he's) play**ing** | He **isn't** playing | **Is** he playing? |
| You/We/They **are** (we're) play**ing** | We **aren't** playing | **Are** they playing? |

**Respuestas cortas:** *Are you eating? → Yes, I **am**. / No, I**'m not**.* · *Is she sleeping? → Yes, she **is**. / No, she **isn't**.*

## Reglas para añadir -ing

- Normalmente se añade **-ing**: *read → reading*, *play → playing*.
- Verbos acabados en **-e**: se quita la **e** → *dance → dancing*, *write → writing*, *make → making*.
- Verbos cortos acabados en **vocal + consonante** (con el acento al final): se **dobla la consonante** → *swim → swimming*, *run → running*, *sit → sitting*, *stop → stopping*.
- Verbos acabados en **-ie**: cambian a **-ying** → *lie → lying*, *die → dying*.

## Present simple o present continuous

| Present simple | Present continuous |
|---|---|
| Rutinas y hábitos. | Lo que pasa **ahora**. |
| *I **play** football **every Monday**.* | *I **am playing** football **now**.* |
| Palabras clave: *every day, always, usually, never, on Mondays* | Palabras clave: *now, at the moment, look!, listen!* |

> 💡 Algunos verbos casi **nunca** van en continuous: **like, love, hate, want, know, understand**. Decimos *I **like** pizza*, no *"I am liking"*.

# Examen

? Completa: Look! The baby ___ (sleep).
+ is sleeping
- sleeps
- are sleeping
- sleeping
> Look! indica que ocurre ahora. Con the baby (it) se usa is.

? ¿Cómo se escribe "swim" + -ing?
= swimming
> Se dobla la m: vocal + consonante al final.

? ¿Cómo se escribe "dance" + -ing?
+ dancing
- danceing
- dancking
- dancting
> Se quita la e final.

? Elige la forma correcta: We ___ watching a film right now.
+ are
- is
- am
- be
> Con we se usa are.

? Elige la pregunta correcta.
+ Is she reading a book?
- Does she reading a book?
- She is reading a book?
- Is she read a book?
> Se pone is/are/am delante del sujeto.

? Respuesta corta: "Are you listening?" — "Yes, I ___."
= am
> Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

? Elige la oración correcta.
+ I play football every Monday.
- I am playing football every Monday.
- I playing football every Monday.
- I plays football every Monday.
> "Every Monday" indica una rutina: present simple.

? "They aren't running" está en negativa.
= verdadero
> Aren't = are not.

? ¿Cómo se escribe "run" + -ing?
= running
> Se dobla la n.

? "I am liking chocolate" es una oración correcta.
= falso
> El verbo like casi nunca va en continuous. Se dice: I like chocolate.
